# PUBG Guides — pubgbattlegrounds.github.io

An unofficial fan-made guide website for PUBG: Battlegrounds. Professional guides for every map, weapon rankings, recoil control, best PC and mobile settings, sensitivity, rank push and endgame strategy — plus 4 free in-browser tools.

**Live site:** https://pubgbattlegrounds.github.io/

## What's inside

- **20 original guides** in `guides/` — Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok and Vikendi map guides, best weapons 2026, recoil control, PC/mobile settings, sensitivity, rank push, solo vs squad, loot, vehicles, grenade and smoke tactics, zone rotations, aim drills, audio settings, crosshair placement, attachments, and how to win a chicken dinner.
- **4 working tools** in `tools/` — sensitivity/eDPI converter, weapon damage calculator, interactive recoil guide, drop distance calculator. All vanilla JS, run fully in the browser.
- **20 article images** in `images/` — AI-generated, one per guide.
- **Pages:** About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer.
- **Official downloads only:** the site links to the official Steam, Google Play and App Store pages for PUBG. No APK links, no third-party downloads.
