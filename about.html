About PUBG Guides

PUBG Guides is an unofficial fan-made website built by players, for players. We write practical, honest guides for PUBG: Battlegrounds — the maps, the weapons, the settings and the strategy that actually wins matches.

What We Do

Our guides cover every part of the game: landing spots on Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok and Vikendi, weapon rankings, recoil control, PC and mobile settings, sensitivity, rank pushing, rotations and endgame play. We also build free in-browser tools — a sensitivity converter, a weapon damage calculator, an interactive recoil guide and a drop distance calculator — so you can plan your game before you even launch it.

How We Write

  • Tested in real matches. Every tip comes from hundreds of hours of actual gameplay, not theory.
  • Updated regularly. Weapon stats, maps and settings are checked against the current version of the game.
  • Fair play only. We never cover cheats, hacks, aimbots or exploits. Everything here is legitimate skill and game knowledge.
  • Written for everyone. Whether it is your first drop or your thousandth chicken dinner, our guides meet you where you are.
