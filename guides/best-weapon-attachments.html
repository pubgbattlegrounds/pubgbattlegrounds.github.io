Best Weapon Attachments in PUBG: Build the Perfect Gun

PUBG: Battlegrounds Guide • Updated November 2026 • 7 min read

A bare M416 and a fully kitted M416 are different weapons. The bare gun kicks, climbs and holds only thirty rounds; the fully attached version sprays smoothly and holds forty. Attachments are the difference between a gun that fights you and a gun that works with you. This guide explains every attachment type, which ones are best, and the exact builds for the most popular weapons.

Muzzles: Compensator, Suppressor and Flash Hider

The muzzle sits on the end of the barrel and shapes how your gun behaves when you fire. The compensator is the best choice for most players: it reduces both vertical and horizontal recoil, making your spray tighter and easier to control. Use it on the M416, the Beryl M762 and the AKM whenever you find one. The suppressor hides your muzzle flash and muffles your shots, making it much harder for enemies to find you. It suits snipers best, but slightly reduces bullet velocity. The flash hider sits in the middle: it hides the flash without the velocity penalty, but does less for recoil than the compensator.

As a rule, take the compensator for close and medium-range fights, and the suppressor for snipers and long-range rifles. In the final circle, a suppressor is extremely valuable because it hides your position. If you must choose on your main rifle, take the compensator; recoil control wins more fights than stealth does.

Grips: Which One Fits Your Style

Grips attach under the barrel and control how your gun moves during sustained fire. The vertical foregrip reduces vertical recoil, the main problem for most players, and is the safest all-round choice for the M416 and the Beryl. The half grip reduces both vertical and horizontal recoil slightly and improves recoil recovery, suiting players who spray in bursts. The angled foregrip improves aim-down-sights speed, which suits aggressive players. The lightweight grip reduces sway, which helps snipers. There is no single best grip: the vertical foregrip is the default, the half grip the most balanced, and the angled grip for speed.

Pro Tip: Test grips in training mode with a full spray at 50 metres. The grip that gives you the tightest pattern is the right one for you.

Scopes and Sights

Your sight decides how well you hit targets at different ranges. A red dot or holographic sight is best for close range: it gives a clear view and lets you track fast-moving enemies. A 2x scope is a good compromise for medium range, while a 3x or 4x scope is ideal for the medium-to-long fights that decide most matches. A 6x scope is versatile because you can adjust it down to 3x. An 8x scope belongs on bolt-action snipers like the Kar98K and the AWM, where it turns long-range shots into reliable kills.

Match your scope to your weapon's job. An 8x scope on an M416 makes close fights nearly impossible, and a red dot on a Kar98K wastes its long-range power. A strong all-round setup is a red dot on your close-range weapon and a 4x or 6x scope on your medium-range weapon. Remember to adjust your scope sensitivity for each magnification, since a 6x scope needs a lower sensitivity than a red dot.

Magazines and Stocks

Extended magazines are the most underrated attachment in the game. An extended magazine adds ten rounds to your M416, taking it from thirty to forty, and those extra bullets win fights constantly: the player who has to reload first usually loses. An extended quickdraw magazine is even better, speeding up reloads too. Always prioritise an extended magazine over a minor grip upgrade.

Stocks reduce recoil and improve stability. The tactical stock is the best option for the M416. The cheek pad, which fits the Kar98K and other snipers, reduces sway and speeds up scoping. The bullet loops on the Kar98K speed up reloads. Stocks are small upgrades alone, but with a compensator and a good grip they turn a good gun into a laser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the compensator always better than the suppressor?

Not always. The compensator is better for recoil control in close and medium-range fights, which is where most gunfights happen. The suppressor is better for snipers and for the final circle, where hiding your position matters more than recoil. Use the compensator on your main rifle and the suppressor on your sniper rifle for the best of both.

Which grip should I use on the M416?

The vertical foregrip is the safest choice because it reduces vertical recoil, which is the M416's main weakness. The half grip is a close second and suits players who fire in bursts. Try both in training mode and pick the one that gives you the tighter spray pattern.

Should I pick up attachments for weapons I do not have?

Yes, if you have backpack space. Attachments are light and you will often find the matching weapon later in the match. Prioritise extended magazines, compensators and scopes, and drop them only if you need the space for healing items or throwables.
