Best Weapons in PUBG 2026: Top Guns Ranked

PUBG: Battlegrounds Guide • Updated November 2026 • 7 min read

Picking the right weapon is one of the biggest decisions in PUBG. The game has dozens of guns, and the difference between a good and a bad choice can decide a fight before it starts. Some weapons are easy to control and forgiving, others hit hard but need practice, and a few are only worth taking in specific situations. This ranking covers the best weapons in PUBG in 2026, based on damage, recoil, fire rate, versatility and how easy they are to use. We have split them into tiers so you can see at a glance what to grab and what to leave behind.

S-Tier: The Best Guns in the Game

The M416 sits at the top of the list for a reason. It has low recoil, a fast fire rate, five attachment slots and solid damage, which makes it the most versatile assault rifle in the game. With a compensator, a vertical grip, a tactical stock and an extended magazine, it can spray accurately at mid range and hold its own up close. The Beryl M762 is the damage king: it fires 7.62mm rounds that hit harder than the M416, but its recoil is stronger, so it suits players who can control a spray. The AWM is the most powerful sniper rifle in PUBG, capable of one-shotting a level 3 helmet, but it only comes from air drops and uses rare .300 Magnum ammo. The Groza, also an air drop exclusive, is a monster at close range with very high damage and a fast fire rate. If you find any of these four, build your loadout around them.

S-tier quick list

  • M416: the most versatile and easiest to control assault rifle.
  • Beryl M762: highest damage assault rifle, harder recoil.
  • AWM: one-shot sniper, air drop only.
  • Groza: devastating close-range air drop rifle.

A-Tier: Reliable and Powerful

The AKM is a classic. It hits as hard as the Beryl but fires more slowly, which makes its recoil easier to manage in bursts. The SCAR-L is a forgiving 5.56mm rifle that works well for newer players. The SLR is the best DMR for most players: it deals heavy damage at range and is easier to use than the Mk14. The Mini14 is the easiest DMR to control and a great choice for long-range fights on Miramar. The Kar98k remains a strong bolt-action sniper that can one-shot a level 2 helmet, and it is far more common than the AWM. The Vector is the best SMG in close quarters, with an extremely fast fire rate, while the DBS shotgun can end a fight in a single well-placed blast inside a building.

Pro Tip: Do not judge a weapon by its damage alone. A gun you can control wins more fights than a gun with bigger numbers. If you cannot control the Beryl's recoil, the M416 will get you more kills, and that is what matters.

B-Tier and Situational Picks

The UMP45 is a dependable SMG that is easy to control and common everywhere, making it a great early-game weapon. The SKS is a solid DMR if you cannot find an SLR, and the DP-28 is a surprisingly strong light machine gun for holding positions, thanks to its large magazine and low recoil when prone. The S12K shotgun is useful in the first minutes of a match, and the Win94 can be fun on Miramar, but neither should be your endgame weapon. The crossbow and the flare gun are niche picks: the crossbow is silent and fun but slow, while the flare gun calls in an air drop that can give you an AWM or a Groza if you use it inside the zone. Weapons to avoid in the late game include the pistols, the Tommy Gun without attachments, and the VSS, which has low damage and a short effective range.

Building Your Loadout

The ideal loadout in 2026 is an assault rifle for close and mid range plus a DMR or sniper for long range. A good example is the M416 with a red dot and the SLR with a 4x scope, which covers every distance. If you prefer aggressive play, swap the DMR for a Vector or a Beryl. Always carry enough ammo: around 200 rounds for your main rifle and 60 to 80 for your secondary. Prioritise attachments in this order: a compensator or suppressor, a vertical or half grip, an extended magazine, and a scope that matches your range. A fully kitted M416 beats a bare Beryl every time, so spend a few extra seconds looting attachments before your next fight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best gun in PUBG 2026?

The M416 is the best all-round weapon thanks to its low recoil, versatility and five attachment slots. The AWM is the most powerful single weapon, but it only comes from air drops.

Is the Beryl M762 better than the M416?

The Beryl deals more damage per shot, but its recoil is much harder to control. Skilled players who can manage the spray often prefer the Beryl, while most players get more kills with the M416.

Which weapon is best for beginners?

The M416 and SCAR-L are the best beginner rifles because their recoil is easy to control. Pair one with a UMP45 or Mini14 and you have a forgiving loadout that works at every range.
