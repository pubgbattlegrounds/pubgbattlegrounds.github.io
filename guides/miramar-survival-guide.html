Miramar Survival Guide: Master the Desert Map

PUBG: Battlegrounds Guide • Updated November 2026 • 7 min read

Miramar is the largest-feeling map in PUBG, and the hardest to survive. It is an 8 by 8 kilometre desert with wide open plains, rocky canyons, steep hills and small towns separated by long stretches of nothing. There is very little cover between compounds, which means long-range fights decide most matches. If you come from Erangel expecting to hide behind trees, Miramar will punish you. The players who do well here pick smart landing spots, master the DMR and sniper rifles, carry plenty of healing, and rotate early in a vehicle. This guide covers everything you need to survive the desert and turn its open spaces to your advantage.

Where to Land on Miramar

Los Leones is the biggest city on the map and the best loot in the desert. Its tall buildings, warehouses and the construction site hold assault rifles, DMRs, level 3 gear and 8x scopes, but it draws several squads every match and fights stretch across rooftops and long streets. If you want action and great loot, land on the northern edge of the city so you can escape toward the hills if the fight goes badly. Pecado is smaller and faster: the casino and the boxing ring are the classic hot drop, with two or three squads landing in a very small area. It is great practice for close-quarters combat, but you will rarely leave with a full loadout. For a safer start, try El Pozo, San Martin or Monte Nuevo. These towns have good loot, fewer enemies and vehicles nearby. El Azahar and Los Higos in the north are quiet and work well for rank pushing. Whatever you pick, land close to a vehicle spawn. On Miramar, a car is not a luxury; it is your only reliable way to cross the open ground between towns.

Landing priorities

  • Land on a roof with a clear weapon spawn and a quick escape route.
  • Grab any rifle or DMR first, then armour, then a scope.
  • Mark a vehicle on the map before you finish looting so your squad knows where to go.
  • Avoid the open desert on foot in the first two minutes; you are an easy target for snipers.

Master Long-Range Combat

Most Miramar fights happen at 100 to 300 metres, so your loadout should reflect that. A DMR like the SLR, Mk14 or Mini14 paired with a 4x or 6x scope is the desert meta. The SLR hits hard and is common on this map, while the Mini14 is easier to control at range. For close range, keep an assault rifle such as the M416 or Beryl M762 for clearing buildings. Always carry a 4x scope at minimum. When you take a long-range fight, fire from behind a rock or a ridge, and move after every few shots so enemy snipers cannot pin your position. Never stand still in the open to heal; use the ridge line or the shadow of a building instead.

Pro Tip: Miramar's ridges are your best friends. Fight from the top of a ridge with only your head and weapon showing, and you can spot enemies crossing the open ground long before they see you. After every knock, drop back behind the ridge to reload and heal safely.

Vehicles and Rotations

Rotations decide more matches on Miramar than gun skill does. The map is so open that running to the zone on foot is slow and dangerous, and the blue zone hits hard. Grab a vehicle as soon as your basic loot is done: the Mirado is fast on roads and great for crossing the desert, the pickup truck handles rough terrain better, and the motorcycle is quick but leaves you exposed. Drive along ridges and dry riverbeds rather than straight across flat plains, and park behind a rock or a building, never in the open. The final circles on Miramar often land on open ground with almost no cover, so arrive early, take the high ground, and keep a smoke grenade ready to cover your last push. If the zone ends on a hillside, the team on top almost always wins, so fight for the high ground before the circle shrinks.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

The biggest mistake on Miramar is fighting in the open. New players loot a town, run straight toward the zone across a flat plain, and get knocked by a team they never saw. Use ridges, rocks and compounds as stepping stones, and check the horizon with your scope before you move. The second mistake is ignoring vehicles: if you do not have a car by the second circle, you are already behind. The third is carrying the wrong guns. A shotgun and an SMG will lose almost every fight on this map, so swap them for a DMR or a scoped assault rifle as soon as you can. Finally, do not underestimate the terrain: clear each ridge before you drive past it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best weapon for Miramar?

A DMR such as the SLR or Mini14 with a 4x or 6x scope is the best all-round choice, paired with an M416 for close range. Long-range fights dominate the map, so a scoped rifle matters more here than on any other map.

Is Miramar good for beginners?

It is the hardest map for beginners because of the open terrain and long-range fights. Land in quieter towns like El Azahar or Los Higos, loot carefully, and focus on rotations and positioning while your aim improves.

Which vehicle is best on Miramar?

The Mirado is the fastest and best for road rotations, while the pickup truck is better off-road and on hills. Take whichever spawns near your landing spot; having any vehicle matters more than having the perfect one.
