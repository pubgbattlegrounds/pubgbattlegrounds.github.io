Rank Push Tips: Climb to Conqueror Faster

PUBG: Battlegrounds Guide • Updated November 2026 • 7 min read

Climbing the ranked ladder in PUBG is a different game from playing casual matches. In ranked mode you face players who take every fight seriously, the zones hurt more, and a single bad decision can cost you points you spent three matches earning. Reaching Conqueror, the highest rank, takes more than good aim: it takes smart rotations, consistent survival, good teamwork and the discipline to avoid unnecessary fights. This guide covers the strategies that actually move your rank up, from choosing the right mode and map to surviving the late game and earning points efficiently.

How Ranked Points Actually Work

Your rank points come from two sources: placement points and kill points. Placement points reward you for surviving: finishing in the top ten earns far more than finishing in the top thirty, and a chicken dinner earns the most of all. Kill points reward eliminations, but they are capped per match, so chasing kills after the cap is reached earns you nothing and risks your placement. This means survival is worth more than aggression in ranked. A match where you finish third with two kills earns more points than a match where you finish fifteenth with six kills. Play accordingly: your first goal in every ranked match is to reach the top ten alive, and only then should you look for fights. Understand the tiers too. You climb from Bronze through Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master and finally Conqueror, and the higher you go, the more points you lose for a bad finish, so consistency matters more than occasional big games.

Point priorities

  • Survival first: top-ten finishes are your main source of points.
  • Kills second: take safe fights and third-party opportunities, not risky pushes.
  • Never die before the third circle unless the fight is clearly winnable.
  • Play for the win once you reach the final ten; that is where the big points are.

Best Maps and Modes for Rank Push

Erangel is the best map for rank pushing. It is large, has plenty of safe landing spots, and rewards smart rotations more than raw aggression. Land somewhere quiet, loot fully, and rotate early. Miramar works too if you are confident at long range, but its open terrain punishes mistakes. Avoid Sanhok for serious rank pushing: the fast pace and constant third parties make it hard to survive consistently. Play squad ranked with a regular team if you can. A coordinated squad that communicates and revives each other climbs far faster than four solo players, and you can plan strategies together between matches. If you must play solo, play squad mode and fill with random teammates rather than solo mode, because squad ranked gives you more chances to survive bad fights through revives. Avoid playing ranked when you are tired or tilted: two bad matches in a row can undo an hour of progress.

Pro Tip: Set a daily limit for ranked matches, such as five games or two hours. When you reach it, stop, even if you are winning. Fresh focus earns more points than tired grinding, and a tilted player loses points faster than a focused one gains them.

Landing, Looting and Rotating

Your ranked match is decided in the first two minutes. Land in a quiet spot away from the flight path, such as a small village or a farm compound, and loot quickly and completely. You need a rifle, a scope, level 2 armour, healing items and a vehicle before you rotate. Never hot drop in ranked unless your whole squad agrees and you are confident you can win the fights. Once the first circle appears, rotate early and take a strong position inside the next zone. Arrive before other teams, hold the high ground, and let them come to you. Use vehicles for rotations but ditch them before the final circles, since a parked car gives away your position. In the mid-game, avoid fighting unless you have a clear advantage: a numbers advantage, better position or a knocked enemy to finish. Every fight you skip is a fight you cannot lose.

Winning the Late Game

The final circles decide your rank points, so play them carefully. Keep your boost bar full, carry at least two smoke grenades, and save your molotovs and frag grenades for the last two circles. When the zone shrinks, move early to the next safe area and take the best cover available: a rock, a ridge or a building with good sightlines. Do not stay in the open waiting for the zone to move. Watch the kill feed to track how many enemies are left, and count the teams by listening to gunfire directions. In the final three players, patience wins: let the other two fight if they are close together, then clean up the survivor. If you must fight, use your utility first: throw a frag to flush an enemy out of cover, then push with your teammate. Finally, review your deaths. After every ranked session, think about the one mistake that cost you the most points and fix it next time. That habit alone will carry you through Diamond and into Master.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to reach Conqueror in PUBG?

It depends on your skill and how much you play, but most players need several weeks of consistent ranked play with a regular squad. Focus on top-ten finishes rather than kills, and your rank will climb steadily.

Is it better to play aggressive or passive in ranked?

Passive early and aggressive late is the winning formula. Survive to the top ten by avoiding risky fights, then play aggressively in the final circles when placement points are already secured.

Which map is best for rank pushing?

Erangel is the best map for rank pushing because of its size, safe landing spots and rotation-based gameplay. Miramar is a good second choice for strong long-range players, while Sanhok is the hardest to survive consistently.
