Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PUBG Guides collects when you visit this website and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

This website is a static guide site. We do not run user accounts, and we do not ask you to register or sign in.

  • Contact messages: If you email us or use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you provide. We use this only to reply to you.
  • Newsletter sign-up: If you subscribe, we store the email address you provide so we can send you new guides. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Usage data: Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic, anonymous visit data such as pages viewed, browser type and approximate location. This is used only to understand which guides are popular.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. The tools on this site (sensitivity converter, damage calculator, recoil guide and drop calculator) run entirely in your browser — nothing you enter into them is sent to us or stored anywhere.

Third-Party Links

This site links to official stores (Steam, Google Play and the Apple App Store) and may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing them with any information.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security and Your Rights

We take reasonable steps to protect any information you send us, and we never sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. You may ask us at any time to delete any message or email address you have sent us by contacting contact@pubgbattlegrounds.github.io.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when we do, and continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
