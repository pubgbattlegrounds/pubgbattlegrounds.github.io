Drop Distance Calculator

Land exactly where you want to. Measure the distance from the plane's flight path to your drop spot on the map, pick your drop style, and find out when to jump.

Plan Your Drop

Open the map in-game: 1 small grid square = 100 m. Measure from the white flight line to your marker.
If your target is directly under the flight line, leave this at 0. The further to the side it is, the earlier you must jump.

This is an estimator based on community-tested averages: a steep dive covers roughly 500 m horizontally, an angled dive about 900 m, and a full glide about 1,500 m. Actual distances vary with your exact dive angle, so treat the numbers as a guide and adjust with experience. If the target is beyond your style's reach, pick a closer spot or switch to max glide.

How Dropping Works in PUBG

The plane flies in a straight line across the map. You can jump at any point, then steer with your parachute. The key skill is judging the distance: jump too late and you land short of your target; jump too early and you drift past it or land in the open.

  • Steep dive (straight down): the fastest way to the ground — about 25 seconds. Use it for hot drops when you want to land first and grab a gun.
  • Angled dive: dive forward at roughly 45 degrees. A good balance of speed and distance — the default choice for most drops.
  • Max glide (level flight): hold forward without diving. The slowest landing — about 60 seconds — but you can travel around 1,500 m. Use it for far-away quiet spots.
  • Open your parachute late when diving steeply — you fall faster and land sooner. The parachute opens automatically if you wait too long.
Pro Tip: Mark your target before you jump, and watch the plane's position on the minimap. When the plane icon is the jump distance away from your marker, jump — then aim your character at the marker and adjust with small steering movements.
