    Spray tips

      Recoil ratings are 1–10 (10 = hardest to control) and reflect the weapon's base behaviour before attachments. Ratings are a guide based on community testing, not exact in-game values.

      Recoil Control Basics

      Every gun in PUBG kicks up and to the side when you hold the trigger. The fix is always the same: pull your mouse down smoothly while spraying, and learn each gun's pattern.

      • Pull down, don't fight it. Start pulling your mouse down gently as soon as you start firing.
      • Crouch before you spray. Crouching reduces recoil noticeably and makes your spray tighter.
      • Use the right grip. Vertical grips cut vertical kick; angled grips help you aim down sights faster.
      • Burst at range. Beyond 100 metres, fire in short 3–5 round bursts instead of holding the trigger.
      Pro Tip: Practise one gun at a time in the training grounds. Spray a full magazine at a wall from 50 metres, look at the bullet pattern, then repeat it while pulling down — you will see the pattern tighten within minutes.
