eDPI = DPI × sensitivity. It is the only fair way to compare sensitivity between players using different mice. cm per 360° is calculated using PUBG's standard base yaw of 0.0022° per mouse count.

How to Use This Converter

If your mouse runs at 800 DPI with a sensitivity of 30, your eDPI is 24,000. Move to a 1600 DPI mouse and you need a sensitivity of 15 to keep the exact same feel — the converter does this maths for you.

  • Low eDPI (below 15,000): slow, steady aim. Great for long-range precision, but you need a large mousepad.
  • Medium eDPI (15,000 – 30,000): the balanced range most players use. Good control with enough speed to react.
  • High eDPI (above 30,000): very fast turning. Good for close quarters, but harder to control at range.
Pro Tip: Change only one thing at a time. If you change DPI, use this converter to match your old sensitivity, then play at least 10 matches before deciding it feels wrong. Your muscle memory needs time to adjust.
