Damage values are based on published PUBG weapon stats. Headshots deal 2.35× base damage (2.5× for the AWM, 1.5× for shotguns) and limb shots deal 0.5×. Helmets and vests reduce damage by 30% / 40% / 55% at levels 1 / 2 / 3. Actual damage falls off with distance, and shotguns depend on how many pellets hit.

How Armour Works in PUBG

A level 3 vest cuts body-shot damage by 55%, which turns a 3-shot knock into a 6 or 7-shot knock for most assault rifles. That is why fights against geared enemies feel so much longer — and why headshots matter so much.

  • Helmets only protect the head. A level 3 helmet is the only thing that stops an AWM headshot from knocking you instantly.
  • Vests only protect the torso. Limb shots ignore armour completely, but they only deal half damage.
  • Armour breaks. Every hit wears armour down, so a vest that has taken damage protects less over time.
Pro Tip: Against a level 3 helmet, stop aiming for the head with an assault rifle — body shots through a level 3 vest still deal more damage per hit than headshots through a level 3 helmet in many cases. Use this calculator to check your own gun.
